# Overview & Design Highlights

The **KR4203 Series** 4.20mm pitch connector system delivers reliable, high-current interconnect solutions for power supplies, industrial automation, and automotive electronics.

## Features & Benefits
* **Positive Locking Mechanism**: Secure latching design ensures high retention force against intense vibration.
* **Fully Isolated Terminals**: Protects contacts from potential handling damage during assembly.
* **Direct Drop-in Replacement**: 100% pin-to-pin and footprint compatible with global Tier-1 industry standards.

## Typical Applications
* Industrial Control Cabinets & Power Distribution
* Telecommunication & Server Power Units
* Automotive Lighting & Dashboard Subsystems
