# Introduction to Micro-Pitch Connectors

In modern electronics manufacturing, miniaturization demands reliable high-density connectors. Wire-to-board and board-to-board connections require high retention force and optimal electrical performance.

## Key Selection Criteria

1. **Current Rating & Contact Resistance**: Ensure terminal alloys can withstand peak current.
2. **Locking Mechanism**: Positive latching systems prevent accidental disconnection in vibrating environments.
3. **Cross-Compatibility**: Standardized pinouts compatible with global market equivalents.
